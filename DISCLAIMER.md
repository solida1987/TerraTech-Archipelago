# Disclaimer

**This is a fan project.** It is not made, endorsed, supported or approved by
Payload Studios, and it is not affiliated with them in any way. TerraTech is
their game and their trademark.

**You need your own copy of TerraTech.** This project distributes no part of
the game and never will. It is a mod that runs inside the copy you bought, plus
a world file for Archipelago.

**It modifies a running game in memory.** The mod uses Harmony to change how
some of TerraTech's own methods behave while you play. It writes nothing into
the game's own files, and removing the mod folder returns everything to normal.
Your saves are untouched: the mod keeps its own bookkeeping in a separate file.

**It may break when TerraTech updates.** The mod hooks methods that Payload
Studios never promised to keep. When an update moves one, the mod says so at
start-up and turns itself off rather than running half-patched — your game
still works, the Archipelago part simply does not until we catch up.

**Nothing here is downloaded on your behalf without asking.** London tells you
what it is about to fetch and from where, every time.

**Multiplayer:** the lock on blocks is network-synchronised, but co-op has not
been tested. Treat it as unsupported until it says otherwise here.

If any of that is not acceptable for your copy of the game, do not install it.
