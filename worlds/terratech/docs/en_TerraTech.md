# TerraTech

## What is this game?

TerraTech is a physics sandbox about building vehicles out of blocks and
driving them into other people's vehicles. You start with a scrappy little
prospecting rig and end up commanding something that should not legally be
called a car.

## What does randomisation do to it?

Every block type is locked. You can find them, buy them and carry them — but
you cannot attach a block until the multiworld sends you the right to use it.

The game's own progression is untouched: you still run the campaign, still earn
licence grades, still face enemies that get meaner as you do. What changes is
that the block catalogue is opened up completely, so anything can be bought
from the start, and Archipelago becomes the only thing standing between you and
bolting it on.

## What items and locations get shuffled?

**Items** are the right to use each of TerraTech's 1144 blocks, plus
corporation licence grades. Filler arrives as money, block packs and supply
crates — delivered, appropriately, by a crate falling out of the sky.

**Locations** are the first pickup and first attach of each block type, the
licence grades, Archipelago blocks placed in vendor stock and mounted on enemy
techs, crates dropped for the purpose, kill and gather milestones, and the
game's own campaign missions.

## Which of my items can appear in other players' worlds?

All of them.

## What does another world's item look like in TerraTech?

A crate falls out of the sky and you drive over to open it.
